01Why

Why I’m doing this

Someone close to me has POTS. I build software for a living, so I went looking for tools that might help her.

I found about eight POTS apps for iPhone. Each has five to ten reviews. In other words, almost nobody uses them.

I have guesses about what might help, and I don’t trust them. So before I write any code, I want to listen to people who live with this.

02The reading

What I’ve learned so far

5y 11m

Average time to diagnosis. Only 1 in 4 people are diagnosed in the first year.

27%

Saw more than ten doctors before being diagnosed.

59%

Were told by a doctor their symptoms were “all in your head.”

69%

Got an anxiety diagnosis before the correct one.

5×

Rise in new cases after the pandemic. Around 30% of people with long COVID meet the criteria for POTS.

50–60%

Are unable to work. 70% have lost income.

From large patient surveys and published research. I’m happy to send you the sources.
